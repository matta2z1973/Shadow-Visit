Get your calendar link from Outlook

This takes about two minutes. You're publishing your class schedule so Shadow Visit always knows when you're free — you only have to do it once.

Do this on a computer, in a web browser, signed in to Outlook with your Greenhill account — not the Outlook phone app or the desktop app. The option you need only lives in Outlook on the web.

Open Outlook on the web

Go to outlook.office.com and sign in with your Greenhill email.

outlook.office.com
📥 Mail
📅 Calendar

Open Settings

Click the gear icon (⚙) in the top right, then “View all Outlook settings” at the bottom of the panel.

Find Shared calendars

In the Settings panel: Calendar → Shared calendars.

Publish your calendar

Under “Publish a calendar,” pick your calendar, then set permissions to “Can view titles and locations.”

Publish a calendar
Select a calendar Calendar ▾
Select permissions Can view titles and locations ▾
Publish
⚠ Not “Can view when I’m busy” — that one hides your class names, which is the whole point.

Copy the ICS link — not the HTML one

Two links appear after you publish. You want the one that ends in .ics.

Calendar — Can view titles and locations
HTML:  https://outlook.office365.com/owa/calendar/…/calendar.html NOT THIS
ICS:  https://outlook.office365.com/owa/calendar/…/calendar.ics COPY THIS

Right-click the .ics link and choose Copy link. Your own links will have a long jumble of letters and numbers in the middle — that’s normal, and it’s what makes the link yours.

⚠ Treat this link like a password: anyone who has it can see your class schedule. Only paste it into Shadow Visit.

Paste it into Shadow Visit

Back on your My interests page, paste the link into the My schedule box and click Save. You’ll see a confirmation once it’s found your schedule.

If something's not working

I don’t see “Shared calendars” anywhere
Make sure you’re in a web browser at outlook.office.com, not the Outlook app on your phone or computer — this setting only exists in the web version.
Shadow Visit says the link isn’t a calendar
You probably copied the HTML link instead of the ICS one — go back to the Publish a calendar screen and copy the other link (it ends in .ics).
I saved it, but nothing showed up
Double check you picked “Can view titles and locations” and not “Can view when I’m busy” when you published — the busy-only option doesn’t share your class schedule.
That’s it — one link, one time. Thanks for hosting!
